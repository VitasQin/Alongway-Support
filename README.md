## 常见问题解答 / Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. 我的数据是如何存储的？ / How is my data stored?
**中文：** “沿途”的所有数据（包括文字、图片、音频）都存储在您的设备本地。如果您在设置中开启了“iCloud 同步”，数据会安全地加密同步到您私人的 iCloud 空间。开发者无法获取您的任何数据。
**English:** All your data (including text and audio) is stored locally on your device. If you enable "iCloud Sync" in the settings, your data is securely and privately synced to your personal iCloud space. The developer cannot access any of your data.

### 2. 为什么重装 App 后音频无法播放？ / Why can't audio play after reinstalling the App?
**中文：** 请确保您在重装前已经开启了 iCloud 同步，并耐心等待 iCloud 自动将音频文件下载到本地。如果仍然无法播放，请在系统设置中检查您的 iCloud 储存空间是否已满。
**English:** Please ensure you had iCloud sync enabled before reinstalling, and wait patiently for iCloud to automatically download the audio files to your local device. If it still doesn't play, check if your iCloud storage is full in the iOS Settings.

### 3. 如何导出我的数据？ / How do I export my data?
**中文：** 您可以在“我的”页面点击“导出数据”，应用会将您的纯文本节点生成为 Markdown (.md) 文件以便保存。注意：音频文件暂不支持直接导出到本地文件夹。
**English:** You can click "Export Data" on the "Profile" page to generate a Markdown (.md) file containing your text nodes. Note: Audio files currently cannot be directly exported to local folders.

### 4. 面容/指纹解锁失败怎么办？ / What if Face ID / Touch ID fails?
**中文：** 如果生物识别失败，应用会自动进入系统密码输入界面。如果您想彻底关闭该功能，请前往 iOS 的“设置 -> 沿途”中关闭面容 ID 权限，或在 App 内的“我的”页面关闭锁定开关。
**English:** If biometric authentication fails, the App will prompt you for your device passcode. To disable this feature, go to iOS Settings -> Alongway to revoke Face ID permission, or toggle off the lock switch in the App's Profile page.
